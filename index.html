What I cut, and why

  • “How do you feel about your weight?” — merged into “What brings you here today?”. It is the same question one screen apart: the first asks the intent, the second asks the intent again with more options. One screen of drop-off for a duplicate answer. Want it back? Say so — it is already wired: it would set a tone variable (“wants muscle” / “wants loss” / “wants maintenance”) that renders on the loader line 2 and as its own row in the “Built from your answers” block on the payoff screen, then swaps the paywall’s lead benefit row. Restored and consumed, not just put back.

What I did not cut

  • Consent Level 1 stays exactly where it is. It is a legal gate on processing health data, not a conversion screen — moving it would be advice about their compliance, not their funnel.
  • “How did you discover Lifesum?” is real attribution value, so it moved past the paywall rather than being deleted. They keep the data, it stops costing pre-paywall momentum.
  • Every body-basics question (gender, birth date, height, current weight, goal weight, activity, pace) — all seven feed the 1,712 kcal target and the goal date. They earn their place, and the wheel picker is the best control in the flow.

What I could not build without you

  • The trial length. Apple's listing tags all three Lifesum SKUs “Trial” but does not publish the duration. The offer paywall says “Today — $0 charged” and “Before your trial ends” rather than inventing “7 days”. Give me the number and it becomes a dated timeline.
  • The 1,712 kcal target and the goal date are rendered as arithmetic on screen (start − target ÷ pace), matching the figure Lifesum's own daily-target screen shows. The real plan engine is theirs.
  • “5x more likely to achieve lasting results” is Lifesum's own paywall claim, kept verbatim. I did not generate it and I have not verified the study behind it — worth confirming before this goes in front of a prospect.
